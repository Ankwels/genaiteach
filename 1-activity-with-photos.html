Freedom Technologist
or Slacktivist?

Six real activists. In pairs, decide: is each one a Freedom Technologist, a Slacktivist, or neither? Use the buttons to record your verdict, then justify it to your partner.

Freedom Technologist
Slacktivist
Neither / It's complicated
Postill's definition: A Freedom Technologist combines political awareness with technical skill to do the sustained, invisible work that keeps movements alive. They are not just people who post — they build platforms, write code, coordinate networks, and connect local action to global audiences. A Slacktivist posts or signs petitions but does nothing that requires real commitment, risk or skill.

Class Discussion

  • Which case was hardest to classify? Why?
  • Does Postill's definition leave room for ordinary people, or does it only recognise experts?
  • Can a Slacktivist become a Freedom Technologist? What would that take?
  • Who is doing the Freedom Technologist work for movements you follow right now?
