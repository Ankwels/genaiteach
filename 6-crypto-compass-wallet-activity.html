Custodial / Hot / Centralized

They control your keys
Easy to use, but you don't truly own your crypto. Company can freeze your account. Higher risk of hacks but convenient for beginners.

Non-Custodial / Cold / Self-Sovereign

You control your keys
More secure and truly yours, but requires technical knowledge. If you lose your keys, you lose everything. Full responsibility.

Crypto Compass Glossary
Custodial
A third party (company) holds and manages your private keys for you
Non-Custodial
You hold and manage your own private keys directly
Hot Wallet
Connected to the internet; convenient but less secure
Cold Wallet
Offline storage; more secure but less convenient
Hardware Wallet
Physical device that stores keys offline (cold storage)
Private Key
Secret code that proves ownership and allows spending
Exchange
Platform where you can buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies
Self-Custody
Taking full responsibility for storing your own crypto
